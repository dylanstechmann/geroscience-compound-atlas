# Data Licenses and Attributions

This repository relies solely on publicly accessible databases and open-source scientific software. No proprietary, patient, or gray-market vendor datasets are incorporated.

## Upstream Sources

### 1. PubChem
- **Provider**: National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI / NLM / NIH).
- **Terms**: Open public domain data (NLM data distribution terms). Bulk queries adhere to NCBI E-utilities / PUG-REST rate limits (≤ 5 requests/sec).
- **Citation**: Kim S, et al. PubChem 2023 update. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 2023;51(D1):D1373–D1380.

### 2. ChEMBL
- **Provider**: European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI).
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- **Policy**: Raw database dumps are not redistributed directly in repository history. Extraction scripts and derived, aggregated benchmark tables are distributed with appropriate attribution.
- **Citation**: Gaulton A, et al. The ChEMBL database in 2017. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 2017;45(D1):D945–D954.

### 3. RDKit
- **Provider**: Open-source cheminformatics toolkit.
- **License**: BSD 3-Clause License.
- **Citation**: Landrum G, et al. RDKit: Open-source cheminformatics. https://www.rdkit.org.

### 4. PubMed / Europe PMC
- **Provider**: NCBI / EMBL-EBI.
- **Terms**: Open access articles queried for bibliographic metadata and evidence provenance.
