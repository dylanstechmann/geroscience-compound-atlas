## Changing biological age

**Objective:** A specified organ/function outcome with independent molecular measures, adverse effects and persistence.

**Starting evidence:** Organ signatures and aging-clock results measure different outcomes; they are not interchangeable causal surrogates. [Primary reference](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06802-1); [review and limits](../../studies/oral-targeting-feasibility-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Which organ, functional deficit and retained systemic benefits define success?

**If resolved in support:** Prespecify a functional endpoint and independent molecular endpoint for the declared organ.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Keep the analysis exploratory; a clock shift cannot supply the missing benefit definition.

**Required measurements:** declared organ and functional endpoint; independent molecular measurement; adverse effects and persistence; appropriate comparator and uncertainty.

**Unsupported shortcut:** A lower clock age, SenMayo score or model probability establishes rejuvenation.

**Defined graph comparisons:** No exact graph panel for this program in this snapshot.

## SLU-PP-915 oral improvement

**Objective:** More reproducible intact active-parent exposure with retained ERR activity and intended function.

**Starting evidence:** Mouse oral activity exists; identified products include enzyme-blank products, without human pathway-rate attribution. [Primary reference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41588687/); [review and limits](../../studies/slu915-oral-comparators-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Is parent loss chemical, enzyme-dependent, dissolution-related, or an analytical recovery problem?

**If resolved in support:** Choose a comparison specific to the established limiting process; retain parent/product resolution and ERR isoform response.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Resolve parent recovery and stability before selecting a new substitution or precursor.

**Required measurements:** intact-parent recovery and loss attribution; ERR EC50 and maximal response kept separate; free/tissue exposure and product identity; interpretable oral-route comparison and function.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Microsomal stability, reduced TPSA or an ester label establishes improved human oral exposure.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `slu915:SLU_PP_915`, `slu915:published_10q`, `slu915:published_10r`, `slu915:product_M1`, `slu915:product_M3`, `slu915:product_M4`, `slu915:hypothesis_aniline_2_4_difluoro`, `slu915:hypothesis_aniline_2_5_difluoro`, `slu915:hypothesis_amide_N_methyl`, `slu915:hypothesis_boronic_pinacol_ester`

## SS-31 oral delivery

**Objective:** Systemic intact peptide after oral delivery with retained useful mitochondrial function.

**Starting evidence:** Reference membrane/cell comparators exist; patents predict oral changes. Animal exposure can fail to translate, as SBT-272 sponsor disclosure illustrates. [Primary reference](https://elifesciences.org/articles/75531); [review and limits](../../studies/ss31-analog-audit-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Does the delivery change increase usable intact-peptide exposure while preserving function?

**If resolved in support:** Separate exposure, mitochondrial access and functional response, with source-specific route references.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Compare unchanged-parent delivery with the analog; increased carrier or total-peptide signal cannot establish success.

**Required measurements:** intact peptide distinguished from conjugate/products; oral exposure with a suitable route reference; mitochondrial access and retained function; interpretable comparison to parent.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Patent prediction or membrane affinity establishes an orally effective human peptide.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `ss31:SS31`, `ss31:SPN4`, `ss31:SS20`, `ss31:SPN10`, `ss31:SS31_N_acetyl`, `ss31:SS31_Phe_backbone_N_methyl`

## SS-31 duration

**Objective:** Preserved useful intracellular exposure or longer functional persistence without losing activity.

**Starting evidence:** Human plasma stability and human systemic residence are different experiments; protease resistance alone is not a duration solution. [Primary reference](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/215244s000lbl.pdf); [review and limits](../../studies/oral-targeting-feasibility-2026-10-09/RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md).

**First question:** Is clearance, cell access or persistence of function the limiting property?

**If resolved in support:** Use a mechanism-specific duration comparison and check retained function at interpretable exposure.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Measure relevant elimination and function before assuming a terminal inversion or conjugation fixes duration.

**Required measurements:** cleavage and clearance distinguished; free and intracellular intact species; retained parent-relative function; persistence of function after exposure falls.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Longer plasma half-life or total albumin-associated signal guarantees a longer useful effect.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `ss31:SS31`, `ss31:SS31_terminal_D_Phe`, `ss31:SPN10`, `ss31:SPN10_D_Arg`, `ss31:SPN10_terminal_D_Lys`

## SS-31 manufacturing cost

**Objective:** Lower manufacturing burden for an equivalently specified material, with acceptable impurities and retained function.

**Starting evidence:** An unchanged-parent process patent and a published Tyr comparator provide different comparisons; neither establishes actual savings. [Primary reference](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3160984B1/en); [review and limits](../../studies/oral-targeting-feasibility-2026-10-09/RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md).

**First question:** Which process step drives measured material and purification burden for parent?

**If resolved in support:** Compare unchanged-parent process alternatives with yield, impurity and stability accounting before sequence substitution.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Gather process economics; residue names and nominal step count cannot establish a cheaper equivalent product.

**Required measurements:** process-scale material/yield accounting; purification and impurity control; specified material equivalence; biological equivalence if structure changes.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Replacing Dmt with Tyr proves cheaper, interchangeable SS-31.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `ss31:SS31`, `ss31:SPN4`

## MOTS-c oral development

**Objective:** Intact active species with retained relevant target, cellular and organ responses after oral delivery.

**Starting evidence:** CK2 and tissue-specific response assays exist; K14Q shows that slower reported clearance can coexist with weaker function. [Primary reference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39559755/); [review and limits](../../studies/peptide-delivery-transfer-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Does a defined change retain relevant function and trafficking independently of immunoreactive persistence?

**If resolved in support:** Assess intact exposure and oral delivery while keeping target engagement, trafficking and tissue responses separate.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Retain parent as the delivery comparator; do not advance a persistent but functionally altered peptide as an upgrade.

**Required measurements:** exact active molecular species; target engagement plus cellular/organ function; native-species trafficking and exposure; oral outcome kept separate from injected trial records.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Higher ELISA signal, CK2 affinity alone, or a CB4211 registry entry establishes oral native-MOTS-c benefit.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `peptides:MOTS_c`, `peptides:MOTS_c_K14Q`, `peptides:MOTS_c_N_acetyl`, `peptides:MOTS_c_C_amide`, `peptides:MOTS_c_both_caps`

## Acetylated Epitalon oral development

**Objective:** A specified capped material with reproducible relevant function and measured intact-species exposure.

**Starting evidence:** Parent cell-line telomere findings and public structure references exist; no capped-versus-parent improvement was established in the reviewed sources. [Primary reference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40908429/); [review and limits](../../studies/peptide-delivery-transfer-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Which parent-relative functional effect remains after each single cap?

**If resolved in support:** Evaluate intact exposure and delivery for that declared effect with separate safety endpoints.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Resolve identity and useful function before an oral optimization campaign.

**Required measurements:** free acid versus C-terminal amide versus salt identity; parent-relative functional comparison; intact-species oral exposure; telomere and safety outcomes kept separate.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Capping, smaller peptide size or telomere lengthening establishes human rejuvenation.

**Defined graph comparisons:** `peptides:Epitalon`, `peptides:N_acetyl_Epitalon`, `peptides:Epitalon_C_amide`, `peptides:Epitalon_both_caps`

## Caffeine off-switch

**Objective:** Sustained reduction of relevant stimulant activity after systemic distribution, followed by a separate sleep outcome.

**Starting evidence:** Aqueous hosts, aptamers and bacterial conversion exist; the reviewed evidence does not establish an oral human reversal molecule. [Primary reference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35107278/); [review and limits](../../studies/caffeine-reversal-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Does recognition survive physiological competition and cover relevant active metabolites?

**If resolved in support:** Resolve intact-host systemic availability, brain redistribution and capture/product disposition before sleep reversal claims.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Redesign recognition/coverage; stronger water binding or greater capacity alone is insufficient.

**Required measurements:** physiological free-ligand capture or characterized conversion; active metabolite/endogenous competitor coverage; oral systemic availability and sustained removal; central response and independent human sleep endpoint.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Buffer affinity, a sensor detection limit, caffeine disappearance or a competing antagonist establishes normal sleep.

**Defined graph comparisons:** No exact graph panel for this program in this snapshot.

## Other stimulant or peptide reversal

**Objective:** Reversal for one declared ligand/mechanism in its relevant compartment.

**Starting evidence:** Ligand-specific molecular-container and protein-binder precedents exist; they do not create a universal off-switch. [Primary reference](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06953-1); [review and limits](../../studies/caffeine-reversal-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Which exact stimulant, active metabolites and central/peripheral mechanism must be reversed?

**If resolved in support:** Compare capture, receptor antagonism and catalytic degradation using mechanism-specific endpoints.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Specify the ligand and compartment; molecular size alone cannot rank reversal difficulty.

**Required measurements:** exact ligand and active species; recognition selectivity and stoichiometry; binding/release rates and compartment access; downstream activity reversibility.

**Unsupported shortcut:** Peptide size or one successful host/binder implies easy reversal of another stimulant.

**Defined graph comparisons:** No exact graph panel for this program in this snapshot.

## Rapamycin reproductive selectivity

**Objective:** Retained target-organ benefit with less relevant testicular pathway suppression and improved direct reproductive endpoints.

**Starting evidence:** Complex-selective and tissue-restricted approaches exist; their reviewed results do not establish testicular protection. [Primary reference](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-11174-0); [review and limits](../../studies/rapamycin-selectivity-2026-10-09/README.md).

**First question:** Which target-organ benefit must be retained, at a matched target response?

**If resolved in support:** Compare free exposure/pathway responses in relevant testicular compartments, then reproductive outcomes.

**If unresolved or not supported:** Define retained benefit before selecting an organ-restricted strategy; systemic and local objectives differ.

**Required measurements:** declared retained organ/function benefit; matched target response; testicular cell/compartment pathway exposure; direct sperm, endocrine and reproductive outcomes.

**Unsupported shortcut:** mTORC2 sparing, lower total testis concentration or brain exclusion proves reproductive safety.

**Defined graph comparisons:** No exact graph panel for this program in this snapshot.
